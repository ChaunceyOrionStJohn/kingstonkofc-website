Welcome

Catholic men serving Kingston since 1897

Kingston Council #275 is a council of the Knights of Columbus, serving eight parishes in Kingston, Esopus, Lake Katrine and Rosendale. We are the men you see running the blood drive, collecting coats for children, praying at the Holy Hour, and quietly helping a neighbor who needed it.

What we do

Through charitable giving and the work of our members, the Council supports Knights of Columbus Coats for Kids, Catholic Charities and Peoples Place, the Ulster County Deanery and the Bravo Pregnancy Support Center, and the Immaculate Conception Bazaar.

Across the year we serve at the soup kitchen and the Holy Family Outreach dinners, run the Coats for Folks drive, hold the Soccer Challenge and the children’s essay contest, mark St. Nicholas Day with our Keep Christ in Christmas celebration, and gather for the Communion Mass and breakfast, the summer picnic and the Christmas party.
