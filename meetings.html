Council calendar

Meeting dates

Council meetings are held on the first and third Mondays of each month at 7:00 PM, at Immaculate Conception School, 471 Delaware Avenue, Kingston.

The first meeting of the month is our business meeting. Changes to the schedule are announced at meetings and, when there is time, posted here. In the event of inclement weather, please call (914) 204-4211.

The Council runs family, social, community, youth and council activities across the whole calendar. Regular fixtures include:

  • Communion Mass and Breakfast
  • Coats for Kids and Coats for Folks
  • Summer picnic
  • Blood drive
  • Ministry for the imprisoned
  • March for Life bus trip to Washington, D.C.
  • Immaculate Conception Bazaar
