About the Council

What is Kingston Council #275?

We are a council of the Knights of Columbus, the world's largest Catholic fraternal service organization, chartered in Kingston on October 24, 1897.

The Knights of Columbus was founded in 1882 by a young parish priest, Father Michael J. McGivney, to protect Catholic families from the hardship and prejudice of his day. Fifteen years later, Dr. E. J. Gallagher and a group of Kingston Catholic men joined that mission and formed the 275th council of the Order. We have been at work here ever since.

Our four principles are Charity, Unity, Fraternity and Patriotism.

Who can join

All practical Catholic men at or over the age of 18 are welcome. Our members include men from all walks of life, bringing a vast array of talent and experience. Our activities are focused on promoting family, community, spiritual and council growth.

Come join the world's most influential Catholic men's fraternal organization.

How to join. Visit kofc.org and use promo code BLESSEDMCGIVNEY for a free 12-month membership — then tell us you have joined so we can welcome you at the next meeting. You can also contact us directly or speak to any Knight at your parish.

Why join the Knights

Faith

Grow in your faith alongside other Catholic men — through Holy Hours, Communion breakfasts, the Rosary, and the March for Life.

Family

Council life includes your family. Picnics, dinners, and family activities run through the whole year.

Charity

Put your hands to work where it is needed: coats for children, food for neighbors, blood drives, and support for expectant mothers.

Brotherhood

A council of men who show up for one another — and for the parishes and the city we all share.

Our charitable work

  • Knights of Columbus Coats for Kids and Coats for Folks
  • The community soup kitchen, including the Darmstadt Shelter run by Family of Woodstock
  • Holy Family Outreach dinners
  • Catholic Charities and Peoples Place
  • Ulster County Deanery and the Bravo Pregnancy Support Center
  • Immaculate Conception Bazaar
  • Support for Catholic education in Ulster County
  • Ministry for the imprisoned
