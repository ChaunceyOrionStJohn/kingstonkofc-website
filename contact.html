Interested in joining?

All practical Catholic men aged 18 and over are welcome. You can join online at kofc.org with promo code BLESSEDMCGIVNEY for a free 12-month membership, or simply call the number above and we will walk you through it.

Supporting our work

The Council's charitable work — Coats for Kids, the food pantry, support for expectant mothers, scholarships for Catholic students — is funded by the generosity of our parishes and neighbours. Cheques may be made payable to Kingston Council #275 and mailed to PO Box 1547, Kingston, NY 12402.
